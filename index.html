The project

Land near Dwarka, on the coast

Richwealth is considering acquiring 16 lakh sq. ft. of non-residential land within a larger 72-acre property near Dwarka.

Location highlights

  • Beachfront
  • National highway frontage
  • Approximately 15–16 km from Dwarkadhish Temple

Location highlights are as supplied by the company. Site area, classification, frontage and distance remain subject to verification.

Future vision

Develop a membership-based coastal resort in Dwarka, with a longer-term vision for a Char Dham hospitality network.

For discussion

Ownership proposal

The intention is to hold the property through a company and issue shares to participating investors. The proposed ownership split is:

  • Investors collectively: 60%
  • Company / promoters: 40%

This is an ownership proposal, not a profit or revenue distribution formula. Final share allocation, voting rights, income distribution and the effect of the buyback on ownership still need to be clarified and set out in definitive agreements.
